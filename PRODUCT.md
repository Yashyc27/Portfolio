## Users

Recruiters, engineering managers, and research teams evaluating Yash Chaudhary for AI/ML engineering internship opportunities.

## Product Purpose

A personal engineering portfolio that helps visitors understand Yash's AI/ML, GenAI, and MLOps work, review his experience, and contact him about internship opportunities.

## Positioning

The portfolio presents work across retrieval augmented generation, machine learning operations, computer vision, and full-stack AI systems. These areas are represented in the existing project and experience content.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static multi-page website hosted on GitHub Pages.
- Use semantic HTML, modular CSS, and vanilla JavaScript where interaction is useful.
- Do not introduce React, Next.js, a server, or a build dependency.
- Existing content includes six project case studies, a separate concept-only RAG research proposal, four internships, two education entries, eight listed credentials, contact details, an accessible HTML resume, and a generated downloadable PDF resume.
- Link to matching public project repositories where confirmed. No live demo URLs, certificate scans, or certification verification URLs are supplied. Issuer logos are identifying marks, not certificate images.
- The semantic caching and cost-aware routing topic is concept-only; do not imply implementation, results, metrics, or contribution.
- Claims and project metrics require owner verification before they are strengthened or expanded.
