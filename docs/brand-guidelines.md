# Brand guidelines

## Positioning

**AI/ML Engineer | Generative AI | LLMs | RAG | AI Application Development**

The portfolio should communicate a builder who can take an AI idea through application design, model integration, retrieval, evaluation, and deployment. Keep claims specific to demonstrated work.

## Voice

- Direct, clear, and technically grounded.
- Explain the problem, the engineering decision, and the result in plain language.
- Prefer evidence and concrete implementation details over hype.
- Use first person for ownership; distinguish individual work from team contributions.

## Identity

The visual identity pairs deep charcoal surfaces with restrained electric blue and violet. Space Grotesk supplies a contemporary editorial voice; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for code, labels, and structured metadata. Generous whitespace, thin quiet dividers, and confident type do most of the visual work.

## Color use

- Charcoal backgrounds and surfaces provide the base.
- Electric blue is the main interactive and emphasis color.
- Violet is a secondary accent for supporting highlights and technical diagrams.
- Keep body text near-white and supporting text light enough to remain legible.
- Do not use blue and violet as large decorative gradients or as a substitute for content hierarchy.

## Asset behavior

Use real project captures and genuine portrait photography where available. Use consistent, simple line icons only when an icon clarifies an action or concept. Do not fabricate testimonials, performance measures, credentials, or project outcomes.

## Source of truth

Design tokens and component rules live in `assets/css/tokens.css` and `assets/css/components.css`; full implementation guidance is in root `DESIGN.md`.
